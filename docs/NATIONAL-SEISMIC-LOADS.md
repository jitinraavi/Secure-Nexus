# Indian equivalent-static seismic floor actions

`client/src/lib/nationalSeismicLoads.ts` supplies a bounded Phase 3 source increment for authored IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 horizontal floor actions. It generates loads for a declared principal plan direction; it does not analyze a structural model or establish seismic design compliance.

## Inspected sources and precise scope

The inspected source is the **BIS-authored IS 1893 (Part 1):2016**, sixth revision, available as an [original publication PDF mirror](https://www.nitsri.ac.in/Department/Civil%20Engineering/CSE_202_Chapter_4-1893-part-1-2016-1pdf.pdf) and a [readable transcription of the BIS book and authored amendments](https://studylib.net/doc/27842316/is1893-part1-2016). The PDF text encoding is damaged; the book transcription resolves the actual clauses and equations. Amendment 1 is dated September 2017; Amendment 2 is November 2020, also readable in its [BIS-authored amendment artifact](https://pdfcoffee.com/amendment-no-2-november-2020-to-is-1893-part-1-2016-criteria-for-earthquake-resistant-design-of-structures-pdf-free.html).

Clause 6.4.2 provides `Ah=(Z/2)(I/R)(Sa/g)`, and 7.6.1 gives `VB=Ah W`. Clauses 7.6/7.7.1 restrict the equivalent-static building exception to regular buildings lower than 15 m in Zone II. Clause 6.4.3 also describes regular structures with approximate `Ta<0.4 s`; this implementation conservatively intersects those restrictions. Table 3 gives Zone II `Z=0.10`. In this short-period equivalent-static range, 6.4.2(a) supplies `Sa/g=2.5`. Clause 7.2.2/Table 7 specifies Zone II's minimum horizontal force as 0.7 percent of seismic weight. Clause 7.6.3(a) provides `Qi=VB Wi hi²/sum(Wj hj²)`; 7.6.3(b) obtains storey shear by summing the applicable floor forces.

The [current BIS catalog](https://standards.bis.gov.in/website/standard-details?encryptedId=eyJpdiI6IlE5RDV2VThyL1piSWFyMlFhU0JIcHc9PSIsInZhbHVlIjoiTm1mVzk5VVBnQzk3d1hDeVp3QUpxQT09IiwibWFjIjoiMWJkODBiNGQzYzYzNjJjNGVhOGUwNjRlYjc3ZjU0YTU4MjYwMTNkMTQ4NzljMTIxMDVkMGNiYmI2ZWQ4MDk4YSIsInRhZyI6IiJ9) also lists IS 1893 (Part 1):2025. This module implements only an explicitly adopted 2016 project basis; it does not state that 2016 is the latest publication or silently apply 2025 provisions. The author must confirm that 2016 and the inspected amendment scope remain applicable to the project. Later editions/amendments require their own implementation and acceptance.

## Author contract

`parseNationalSeismicInput(unknown)` accepts version 1 and 1–20 uniquely identified `in-is1893-equivalent-static` checks, with 1–40 floor mass levels per check and at most 200 total levels. Floors must have unique IDs, strictly increasing positive heights measured from the declared base, and a final mass level exactly equal to the declared building height. That final-level condition is a product bound. Seismic weights are already established forces in **N**, not kg; heights are m, period is s, and Z/I/R/Sa/g are dimensionless. Quantities must be finite and within explicit resource bounds. Unknown fields and absent declarations are rejected.

Every check supplies sources for the axis/geometry, method applicability, amended regularity, site/soil, coefficient selections, approximate period and seismic-weight derivation. Z, I, R, Sa/g, period and every floor weight also carry individual quantity sources and optional frame-criterion IDs. Linked criteria must uniquely resolve and exactly match the captured value, unit and source; any referenced adopted standard must resolve uniquely and specify its clause.

The input explicitly records seismic zone, regularity, soil and spectrum kind, ordinary-building scope, ground-level base, basement presence, special dissipation/isolation systems, prestress, long-span amplification, large overhangs, required vertical/dynamic effects and external rigid-diaphragm review. No default is silently inferred. Reviewed `adopted2016ApplicabilityConfirmed`, `amendmentScopeConfirmed` and `amendmentReviewSource` declarations are mandatory. These fields record project-author decisions rather than performing those reviews.

The supported action calculation requires Zone II, regular configuration, height strictly below 15 m, reviewed approximate period strictly below 0.4 s, soil I/II and the code equivalent-static 5-percent-damped spectrum. Supplied Z and Sa/g must exactly match 0.10 and 2.5 in that narrow scope. Ordinary-building, ground-level/no-basement and approved diaphragm declarations are required; special devices/isolation, prestress, long-span amplification, large overhangs and required vertical/dynamic effects are rejected. Restrictions beyond the cited method exception are clearly product bounds, not general statements that BIS prohibits those structures.

Valid declarations outside the implemented scope receive `unsupported-method`, source-specific scope issues, null metrics and no floor forces. Soft/site-specific soils, response-spectrum ordinates, irregular/tall/long-period buildings and required dynamic/vertical procedures are never converted to an apparently accepted static result. The project must separately assess the updated vertical-effect triggers in Amendment 2 and the amended regularity/period provisions. R/I eligibility and full Tables 4/5/6/8/9 classifications remain external; bounded numerical input alone is not a table-eligibility check.

## Adoption and output

`assessNationalSeismic(input,basis)` captures normalized source and complete design basis. Calculation requires all general basis metadata to be complete, country `IN`, the supported reference-catalog version, declared region and jurisdictional authority, named reviewer confirmation, non-empty unique standard/criterion IDs and exactly one matching `in-is1893-1` seismic reference with edition `2016`, publisher reference, local adoption reference and amendments declaration. A linked quantity criterion's adopted standard must additionally match a unique known reference for the captured country, code/domain, edition and publisher; custom or mismatched standard links remain unsupported by this national subset. Wrong country/edition, ambiguous references, incomplete metadata or unresolved criteria return `unsupported-basis` with no Indian member/load evaluation. Issue lists are deduplicated.

Top-level status is `assessed-subset` when all method scopes are supported, `partially-supported-scope` for a mixture, or `unsupported-scope` when no check can generate actions. Unsupported scope results retain their specific issues and source, and an entirely unsupported report has no implemented-clause list.

For an eligible check the module reports Ah, total seismic weight, coefficient base shear, the independent `0.007 W` minimum and adopted base shear `max(Ah W,0.007 W)`. When the minimum is larger, using the height-squared pattern for that minimum is explicitly disclosed as a conservative authored design-action choice. It is not presented as a separate exact BIS distribution rule. The governing basis, weighted-height denominator, force-balance residual and overturning moment about the declared base are recorded.

Floor records contain source weight/height, distribution weight and fraction, equal-opposite positive/negative forces and the storey shear immediately below each floor. Positive and negative records describe separate reversals along the specified principal plan direction. Code plan X/Y labels do not establish an application/world-axis or node mapping. Reconciliation to floor centres of mass, diaphragms, structural nodes and individual resisting elements remains external. The force-conservation tolerance `1e-12` is a numerical product check, not a national-code coefficient.

Reports retain `verification: "unverified"` and `compliance: "not-assessed"`. Results say `generated-floor-actions`, not that a building passes seismic design. Torsion and accidental eccentricity are explicitly excluded even for a regular building; the module never states they are unnecessary. In-plan stiffness distribution, modal/time-history analysis, dynamic scaling, multidirectional/vertical action, soil-structure interaction, drift/stability, member design/detailing and foundations remain excluded.

No gravity/seismic combination generator is added in this increment. The existing authored-load workflow can carry supplied and reviewed combinations, but a national generator must implement the complete applicable material, directional and vertical/torsion contract before claiming coverage.

## Restore and deferred acceptance

`validateNationalSeismicReport(value,source,basis)` is a boolean type guard. It requires strict report fields, exact normalized input/basis identity, adoption/method statuses and issue lists, source-bound closed-form arithmetic for every metric/floor force/story shear, fixed scope warnings/exclusions and exact clause flags. Input/report/basis arrays must be dense; missing entries are rejected. The captured basis, standard, criterion and declaration records reject unknown fields instead of ignoring them. Unsupported cases must keep null metrics and empty actions/checks. The validator never calls the assessment/load-generation routine or a structural/modal solver. Persisted values must retain exact IEEE-754 arithmetic; editing or rounding an action cannot silently change acceptance.

`nationalSeismicExample()` supplies illustrative SI inputs and explicitly labels every review/selection as requiring replacement with checked project sources. It neither creates nor confirms a project adoption basis. Independent numerical benchmarks, source-unit checks, method-boundary rejection, coefficient/minimum force patterns, storey shear/overturning, unique-reference checks and persistence mutation acceptance remain deferred. No app, tests, build, lint/typecheck, preview or repository calculation was executed.
