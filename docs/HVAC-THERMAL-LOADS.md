# Source-bound steady-state HVAC zone thermal duty

`client/src/lib/hvacThermalLoads.ts` supplies a bounded Phase 3 MEP increment for a single-zone steady-state condition. It balances authored envelope transfer, solar/internal heat and moisture gains, and balanced outdoor/infiltration exchange, then maps sensible duty to a separately identified terminal airflow requirement. Optional equipment checks compare simultaneous net-to-zone capacities at the exact captured condition. All national checks remain unsupported; this is a supplied-criteria physical calculation, not complete mechanical design.

## Primary physical references

The EnergyPlus 25.1 [zone/air-system integration reference](https://bigladdersoftware.com/epx/docs/25-1/engineering-reference/basis-for-the-zone-and-air-system-integration.html) identifies sensible air exchange `m cp (Tsource-Tzone)` and the steady-state limit obtained by neglecting air capacitance. The [zone design loads/airflow reference](https://bigladdersoftware.com/epx/docs/25-1/engineering-reference/zone-design-loads-and-air-flow-rates.html) supplies the moisture-flow relationship and sensible load-to-airflow relationship. Its design-day sequences and peak selection are deliberately outside this increment.

The EnergyPlus 25.1 [moisture-balance documentation](https://bigladdersoftware.com/epx/docs/25-1/engineering-reference/indoor-living-wall.html) identifies infiltration moisture `m (wout-wzone)` and humidity ratio as water mass per dry-air mass. The [coil reference](https://bigladdersoftware.com/epx/docs/25-1/engineering-reference/coils.html) relates latent duty to air mass, humidity change and water vaporization heat. The DOE-hosted [envelope technical presentation](https://www1.eere.energy.gov/buildings/publications/pdfs/building_america/ns/eemtg082011_c12_envelope_energy.pdf), “Estimating a Building Envelope’s Energy Performance,” August 10, 2011, gives transmission heat loss proportional to `U*A*temperature difference`.

The implementation applies these physical relationships with externally supplied constant properties. It does not reproduce the EnergyPlus dynamic model or psychrometric functions. No physical default, weather value, national coefficient, gain table, occupancy rate, sizing allowance or equipment curve is embedded.

## Balance and sign convention

All rows describe one sourced design condition. With `deltaT=Tout-Tzone` and `deltaW=wout-wzone`:

- Envelope sensible gain is `UA*deltaT`; the aggregate UA excludes solar and air exchange.
- Each disjoint exterior flow uses dry-air mass `m`. A mass row supplies it directly; a volume row supplies actual volume at outdoor state and dry-air mass per unit of that actual volume, so `m=V*rho_dry`.
- Air-exchange sensible gain is `m*cp*deltaT`; moisture gain is `m*deltaW`; latent-equivalent gain is `hfg*m*deltaW`.
- Net sensible gain is envelope plus separately authored solar/internal sensible gains plus summed air sensible gains. Net latent gain is separately authored internal latent gain plus summed air latent gains.

Positive net gain requires removal: cooling or dehumidification. Negative gain requires addition: heating or humidification. The report retains four separate non-negative duties `max(Qs,0)`, `max(-Qs,0)`, `max(Ql,0)` and `max(-Ql,0)`. It does not cancel a sensible heating need against a latent cooling need. Solar gain is non-negative; internal sensible and latent inputs may represent signed gains or sinks and require explicit sources.

Both components must be compatible with the declared mode. A cooling row with any heating/humidification requirement, or a heating row with any cooling/dehumidification requirement, gives `unsupported-mixed-duty`. Physical signed balances and the four duties remain visible, while selected combined duty, mapped supply flows and equipment comparison remain null. Mixed/reheat/economizer operation is not modelled. Exact zero is treated as no duty without an invented tolerance.

For a supported mode, sensible thermal dry-air supply flow is `Qs_mode/(cp*abs(Tzone-Tsupply))`. The mapped requirement is the greater of that flow and the independently authored minimum total terminal flow. The named `airflowRequirementId` and review source identify its intended air-system connection; this module does not mutate or solve an air network. It is a sensible airflow check, not proof of latent air-delivery feasibility or national ventilation compliance.

## Strict authored input and scope

`parseHvacThermalInput(unknown)` accepts version 1 and 1–40 independent zone/condition/mode rows. Check IDs and `(zoneId,designConditionId,mode)` tuples are unique; a terminal-flow requirement or equipment ID cannot be assigned twice under the same condition and mode. This prevents sharing one capacity across independently checked zones without a system calculation. Each row has at most 16 uniquely identified exterior streams and boundary IDs. Arrays must be dense and bounded; all objects and arrays reject extra fields. All numbers are finite.

Every numeric input is a parameter with `value`, exact SI `unit`, non-empty `source`, optional `criterionId`, and an optional paired `standardId`/`clause`. Air inputs and derived capacities do not receive guessed values. Required sourced inputs are aggregate `envelopeUA`, indoor/outdoor dry-bulb temperatures and humidity ratios, effective constant sensible `cp` on a **dry-air mass basis**, temperature-appropriate water `hfg`, separate solar/internal sensible/internal latent gains, supply dry bulb, and an independent terminal minimum dry-air mass flow.

The exact parameter units are `degC`, `W/K`, `W`, `kg-water/kg-dry-air`, `J/(kg-dry-air*K)`, `J/kg-water`, `kg-dry-air/s`, `kg-dry-air/m3`, and `m3/s`. A volume row must use `actual-volume-at-outdoor-state` and sourced `kg-dry-air/m3`; unspecified total moist-air density and standard-volume data are not interchangeable with it. A mass row rejects volume/density data. Each exterior row explicitly declares equal dry-air outflow at indoor state. No extra outgoing heat/moisture term is added because the differences already include it.

The parser requires authored review declarations for psychrometric feasibility; balanced air exchange; disjoint streams/no duplicate outdoor accounting; gains excluding air exchange; no heat recovery; no interzone transfer; unconditioned exterior exchange at the zone boundary; single-zone terminal duty; and inclusion of all applicable auxiliary effects once in the authored gains. Sources identify design-condition, air-exchange, applicability and airflow-mapping reviews. These declarations do not calculate saturation, auxiliary gains, mass-balance networks, pressure or code ventilation.

Implementation bounds are temperatures `-100..150 degC`, humidity ratios `0..0.2`, UA `0..1e8 W/K`, gains magnitude at most `1e10 W`, dry-air/actual-volume flows `0..1000`, dry-air density `0.01..10 kg-dry-air/m3`, sensible cp `100..10000 J/(kg-dry-air*K)` and hfg `1e5..1e7 J/kg-water`. Supply temperature must have the selected sensible direction and at least `0.01 K` difference. These are numerical bounds, not accepted comfort, material, property or code ranges. Derived quantities must be finite with magnitude at most `1e24`; duties, capacities, flows and non-null utilization are non-negative.

## Optional equipment duty

`equipment` is explicit null or a sourced candidate with `capacityBasis: "net-to-zone-at-exact-design-condition"`, simultaneous-capacity review, sensible/latent capacities and available dry-air supply flow. Its design-condition ID, mode, indoor/outdoor temperatures/humidity ratios, supply temperature and capacity airflow must exactly match the captured row. The capacity flow is the available authored flow. Mismatched conditions are rejected; no interpolation, derating, manufacturer curve or off-design correction is inferred. The source/reviewer must establish that sensible and latent capacities can be delivered simultaneously, with auxiliary interactions included consistently in the gains and net ratings.

Credited sensible capacity is `min(authored sensible capacity, available flow*cp*abs(Tzone-Tsupply))`; airflow cannot be bypassed by a large authored capacity. Latent capacity remains a separately reviewed simultaneous net-to-zone value. Required sensible duty, latent duty and mapped airflow must each fit their available quantities. A candidate passes only when all three fit. This conditional comparison does not prove a feasible leaving humidity, control sequence, fan pressure, whole-system capacity or manufacturer certification.

Zero available capacity/flow fails any positive corresponding requirement. A zero-flow terminal cannot declare nonzero thermal capacities; standalone non-air moisture equipment is outside the subset. Utilization is null for a positive-over-zero requirement and zero when both required and available are zero. A missing candidate yields `loads-and-airflow-only`, with no equipment pass. Results use `within-supplied-duty` or `exceeds-supplied-duty` only for an authored supported candidate. Independent rows are never added into a coincident building/shared-system equipment load.

## Complete selected-country basis and restoration

`assessHvacThermalLoads(input,basis)` captures the exact normalized source and complete strict design basis. Every general basis completeness issue gates calculation, including country/region/authority, current profile, adopted standards/editions/publisher URLs/adoption/amendments, declarations, reviewer confirmation, criterion metadata and unique identifiers. Known catalog standards must match their selected country and catalog metadata. Any complete selected country can use the same physical balance, without receiving national-code equations. Missing or incomplete basis gives `unsupported-basis` and no zone results.

Optional parameter criterion IDs must resolve to exactly one captured criterion with matching value, exact unit, source and optional standard/clause. Zone/property/airflow parameters use module `air`; candidate capacities and available flow use module `equipment`. A direct standard trace must resolve to one complete adopted reference and clause. Unknown, duplicate, cross-country catalog or mismatched criterion traces do not select an arbitrary match. No criterion is silently read by name as an undocumented default.

Reports carry `verification: "unverified"`, `claim: "supplied-criteria-only"`, `nationalCodeCompliance: "not-assessed"`, and implementation `steady-state-zone-thermal-v1`. `nationalChecks` always names the captured country and lists unsupported national ventilation, envelope/climate/energy, comfort/humidity/equipment/installation and full mechanical compliance checks. A mixed-duty result gives `partial-subset`; otherwise a complete basis gives `assessed-subset`, including visible equipment failures. `allSuppliedEquipmentDutiesSatisfied` is null when the basis is unsupported, no equipment exists, or any authored equipment duty has unsupported mode. Otherwise it summarizes only the authored equipment comparisons and does not imply that unassigned zones have equipment.

`validateHvacThermalReport(value,source,basis)` is a captured-report boolean guard. It independently checks source and the complete basis fingerprint, exact basis issues/national flags/warnings/details/exclusions, row identity and direct signed scalar balances, finite bounds, separate duties, supply flow, capacity-flow limitation, utilization null patterns, candidate conditions, statuses and aggregate equipment flag. It never calls the assessor, zone-result routine, solver or any dynamic analysis. Persisted floating-point values must match directly; rounded or edited values cannot create a boundary pass. Generated reports use the same validator before return.

`hvacThermalExample()` is an illustrative cooling snapshot without equipment. All source/review text is marked for replacement. It does not select a country, confirm a basis or imply project weather, humidity, loads or code criteria.

No repository code, application, tests, builds, typechecking, lint, previews, native programs or numerical routines were executed. Later authorized acceptance should include independent signed sensible/moisture balances, dry-mass/actual-volume equivalence with reviewed density, cooling/heating/zero/mixed cases, no double air accounting, exact supply-temperature and equipment-condition gates, independent flow floors, zero/insufficient capacities, selected-country and criterion ambiguity, bounded dense-array/unknown-field rejection and every persisted scalar/status/null-pattern mutation. Dynamic loads, psychrometrics, actual latent air delivery, national standards and complete coordinated MEP design remain outside this increment.
