# US steel E3 flexural compression subset

`client/src/lib/usSteelCompression.ts` adds one bounded Phase 3 national-clause increment. It calculates ANSI/AISC 360-22 E3 flexural buckling resistance for declared nonslender, prismatic, doubly symmetric hot-rolled I sections under pure axial compression. It does not issue a complete column capacity or full US-code compliance result.

## Inspected primary artifact

The implementation follows the AISC-authored **ANSI/AISC 360-22**, August 1, 2022, revised September 2023: [publisher-authored PDF mirror](https://welovesteelconstruction.ssi-steel.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Specification-AISC-360-22_Specification-for-Structural-Steel-Buildings.pdf), with a [readable transcription of the same book](https://studylib.net/doc/27880833/aisc-360-22-16th-edition). The [AISC publication announcement](https://www.aisc.org/news/aisc-releases-new-version-of-specification-for-structural-steel-buildings-ansiaisc-360-22/) establishes the edition and publisher. The original publisher pages were read through cached PDF extraction and the book transcription.

E1, pages 16.1-38/39, supplies `phi_c=0.90` and `Omega_c=1.67`, but requires the least strength among applicable compression limit states. E3, pages 16.1-40/41, gives `Pn=Fn Ag`, `Fe=pi² E/(Lc/r)²`, and `E=200000 MPa`. This module uses `Fn=0.658^(Fy/Fe) Fy` for `Fy/Fe<=2.25`, otherwise `Fn=0.877 Fe`. The 2022 notation is `Fn`. E2 gives `Lc=K L`; its preferred slenderness of 200 is disclosed without converting it into a mandatory capacity limit. B4.1/Table B4.1a classifies compression elements as nonslender or slender; compact/noncompact classification belongs to flexure. The E3 note warns that E4 can govern, including where torsional effective length exceeds lateral effective length.

The [AISC errata listing](https://www.aisc.org/aisc/publications/revisions-and-errata/) includes January 2025 and September 2026 errata. Their contents could not be independently retrieved during this increment. No later errata are silently incorporated or declared irrelevant. A project reviewer must provide explicit amendment and errata applicability declarations establishing that these implemented factors/equations remain applicable to the adopted project. The implementation still reports its inspected artifact revision and limitation.

## Input and adoption contract

`parseUSSteelCompressionInput(unknown)` accepts version 1 and 1–100 uniquely identified `us-aisc360-e3` checks. It rejects unknown fields, non-finite quantities, missing source declarations, non-positive geometry/material/effective-length quantities and unsupported scopes. Section properties are gross area in m² and principal-axis inertias in m⁴. Unbraced lengths are metres, specified yield strength is Pa, effective-length factors are dimensionless, and positive axial compression is N. Each parameter records a source; optional criterion IDs must exactly resolve to unique captured frame criteria with matching quantity, unit and source. Referenced standard IDs require a unique adopted standard, clause, complete edition/adoption/amendment fields and HTTPS source; registered identifiers must match their country, code, domain, edition and publisher.

Each check explicitly declares `sectionType: "rolled-i"`, `prismatic: true`, `doublySymmetric: true`, `nonslenderCompressionElements: true`, and zero moments about both principal axes. `classificationReviewSource` identifies the external B4.1/Table B4.1a review of every compression element. `amendmentScopeConfirmed`/`amendmentReviewSource` and `errataApplicabilityReviewed`/`errataReviewSource` are mandatory author declarations, not an automatic amendment reconciliation.

The input type couples `designMethod: "LRFD"` to `loadBasis: "factored"`, and `designMethod: "ASD"` to `loadBasis: "service"`. The parser also enforces that pairing. The author supplies the applicable analysis and load-combination reference through `loadCase` and `loadSource`; this module does not construct or verify those combinations. Tension, bending, alternate section forms and alternate design methods are rejected rather than silently mapped to compression inputs.

`assessUSSteelCompression(input,basis)` captures normalized source and basis. Calculation requires country `US`, the supported catalog version, project region and jurisdictional authority, named reviewer confirmation, non-empty unique standard/criterion IDs and exactly one matching `us-aisc360` steel catalog reference with edition `2022`, publisher URL, local adoption reference and amendment declaration. Wrong countries/editions, ambiguous references and unresolved linked criteria return `unsupported-basis` with no member results. General basis-completeness findings are also preserved. Country selection never substitutes this US equation for another country's requirements.

## Results and restoration

Each result records the two principal-axis radii, effective lengths, slenderness ratios, Euler stresses, `Fy/Fe` ratios and nominal stress curve choices. The lesser **E3 flexural** stress determines `nominalE3CapacityN`. `availableE3CapacityN` is `0.90 Pn` for LRFD or `Pn/1.67` for ASD. These names deliberately retain the E3 scope. The result includes the supplied demand, utilization, an exact method-specific clause comparison and `within-implemented-clause` or `exceeds-implemented-clause` status.

Every report keeps `verification: "unverified"` and `compliance: "not-assessed"`. E1's complete minimum-over-limit-states assessment, E4 torsional/flexural-torsional buckling, calculated local-slenderness classification, E7, Chapter C/Appendix 7 frame stability and K determination, second-order effects, combined loading/Chapter H, built-up/HSS/composite/non-prismatic members, load combinations, material qualification, connections, serviceability, fatigue and fire remain excluded. Passing this comparison cannot establish a governing column resistance or construction suitability.

`validateUSSteelCompressionReport(value,source,basis)` returns false for malformed/stale records. It requires the exact normalized source and complete basis fingerprint, supported adoption gates, fixed method constants, exact per-axis closed-form source algebra, strengths, utilization, curve choices, governing flexural axis, clause flags and status. Its warning/detail/exclusion lists must also match the implementation, preventing a persisted report from replacing the scope statements. Stored IEEE-754 values must match exactly; rounded or edited strengths cannot introduce a boundary pass. The validator does not call the assessment/member-analysis routine, solve a model or rerun any solver.

`usSteelCompressionExample()` supplies illustrative SI input and clearly marks its classification, adoption, errata and material/restraint declarations as examples requiring replacement with project review. It does not provide or confirm an adopted project basis.

Engineering workspace version 5 stores this input and captured report while recovering versions 1-4 without assessment. Linked-project import copies the project's adopted basis, leaving the module's analytical section/load data explicitly authored. Changing source or basis hides stale results.
