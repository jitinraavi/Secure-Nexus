Privacy & local data · updated 28 October 2026

Understand what you keep here.

This notice explains the account, project and browser data used by Groundwork. Your workspace administrator is the contact for deployment-specific hosting, retention, access and deletion requests.

Account and service data

Creating an account supplies a username, email address, password and selected country. Email verification and sign-in process authentication requests. Optional phone, business and tax details can be added later in Settings for identity and billing. Do not put confidential project information in profile fields.

The service processes designs, uploaded sources, project membership, saved revisions and other content you choose to use. Security and activity records can include event time, device/browser information and network address. These records support account protection, troubleshooting and traceability.

Copies on this device

Groundwork can retain project copies, recovery drafts, source files and queued changes in browser storage. Those local copies and downloaded recovery archives are not encrypted by Groundwork. Anyone with access to this browser profile or an exported archive may be able to read them.

On a shared device, review the sign-out choices and retained data before leaving. Preserve unsynchronized work before choosing removal. Clearing browser storage can remove local recovery copies; it does not delete saved server projects.

Offline application files are separate from private project copies. Preparing the offline shell downloads public application resources. A ready offline shell does not itself grant project access or guarantee that a particular project was saved for offline use.

Uploads, rendering and payments

Sources are sent to the service when you upload or save them. Optional AI/rendering and payment features may use configured external providers. Review the selected feature and provider before submitting sensitive content or payment details. Ask the workspace administrator which providers and regions are configured and what retention rules apply.

Control and requests

Use account settings to review sessions and security controls, project tools to manage your content, and the recovery/export controls to preserve work. For account deletion, server-retention questions, privacy requests or provider details, contact the administrator who operates your workspace through your established support channel. Server retention periods and provider arrangements depend on that deployment; this notice does not promise an unconfigured deletion period.

Use only content you are authorized to upload and share. Review collaborators and public links carefully; exported files and recovery archives are separate copies under the recipient's control.
