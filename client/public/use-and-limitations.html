Service use & output limitations

Make informed use of every output.

Groundwork supports conceptual design and preliminary review. These product limitations explain the supported scope; they do not replace a separate service agreement with your workspace operator.

Before relying on a result

  • Check units, coordinates, source revision, assumptions and supported geometry. Imported or derived geometry may be partial or approximate.
  • Authored CAD top/elevation PDF views and explicitly defined vertical section cuts can use a physical 1:n scale and local cardinal direction. Print at 100% and verify the scale bar. Oversized scaled geometry is rejected; choose another scale or explicit NTS. Top/elevation views show projected edges without hidden-line removal. Sections show supported solids intersected by the declared X/Z plane, without geometry beyond the cut. Review selected levels and coverage notes; generated site/building forms, room shells, furniture and MEP routes are outside these CAD views. NTS views and legacy illustrations are unscaled. Every sheet remains a review output, not a construction approval.
  • Engineering calculations have bounded mathematical scope. A result that satisfies entered criteria is not national-code compliance, approval, certification or a complete design.
  • IFC checks performed inside Groundwork do not establish interoperability with every other application. Verify the receiving tool, supported schema, dimensions, identifiers, properties and coverage.
  • Native DWG conversion, native solver execution and AI rendering depend on configured services. Check live readiness before planning a workflow around them.
  • Have qualified professionals review life safety, structure, MEP, accessibility, fire protection, geotechnical matters and permit submissions as applicable to your project.

Your workspace and shared content

Upload and share only material you are authorized to use. Confirm permissions, recipients and link scope before sharing. Keep an independent copy of important work and review save, synchronization and recovery status before closing a session.

Plan entitlements do not make unavailable workers or externally unverified engineering methods ready for production use. Check the workbench and discuss operator-specific service terms, support, retention and availability with your workspace administrator.
